Taking Your Shahada

The Shahada (testimony of faith) is the entry point into Islam. It must be said sincerely, with understanding and conviction.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah

"I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His messenger."

What it means:

  • La ilaha illallah — Nothing deserves worship except Allah alone
  • Muhammadan rasulullah — Muhammad ﷺ is Allah's final messenger

You can say the Shahada anywhere, anytime — alone or with witnesses. What matters is the sincerity in your heart. Visit your local mosque to make it official, but the moment you believe sincerely, you are Muslim.

Welcome to Islam

Congratulations on your journey! The word "revert" reflects the belief that everyone is born in a natural state of submission to God. Embracing Islam is returning to that fitrah (natural disposition).

This guide provides essential information, resources, and support. Remember — go at your own pace. Consistency matters more than perfection.

The Essentials

🕌 Six Articles of Faith

Belief in Allah, Angels, Divine Books, Prophets, Day of Judgment, Divine Decree.

📿 Five Pillars

Shahada (testimony), Salah (prayer), Zakat (charity), Sawm (fasting), Hajj (pilgrimage).

Shahada

"There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is His messenger." The declaration that makes one Muslim.

Salah

Five daily prayers: Fajr (dawn), Dhuhr (noon), Asr (afternoon), Maghrib (sunset), Isha (night).

Zakat

Annual charity of 2.5% of savings — purifies wealth and supports those in need.

Sawm

Fasting during Ramadan from dawn to sunset — teaches self-discipline and empathy.

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Mecca once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able.

How to Pray (Salah)

Wudu (Ablution)

  • Intention (niyyah)
  • Wash hands 3x
  • Rinse mouth & nose 3x
  • Wash face 3x
  • Wash arms to elbows 3x
  • Wipe head & ears once
  • Wash feet to ankles 3x

Prayer Steps

  • Stand facing Qibla
  • Say "Allahu Akbar"
  • Recite Al-Fatiha + short surah
  • Bow (Ruku)
  • Rise
  • Prostrate (Sujood) 2x
  • Complete rak'ahs
  • Tashahhud & Salam

Prayer Times

  • Fajr: Dawn
  • Dhuhr: Midday
  • Asr: Afternoon
  • Maghrib: Sunset
  • Isha: Night

💡 Tips

  • Start with one prayer at a time
  • Use transliteration
  • Pray with a friend
  • Be patient with yourself

Ghusl (Ritual Bath)

Ghusl is a full-body ritual purification required in specific situations. As a new Muslim, you'll need it after certain impurities.

When is Ghusl required?

  • After marital relations
  • After nocturnal emission (wet dream)
  • At the end of menstruation (women)
  • At the end of postpartum bleeding (women)
  • Before converting to Islam (recommended)

How to perform Ghusl:

  • Make intention (niyyah) in your heart
  • Say "Bismillah" and wash hands 3x
  • Wash private parts
  • Perform Wudu (ablution)
  • Pour water over head 3x, ensuring it reaches roots of hair
  • Wash right side of body 3x, then left side 3x
  • Wash feet last

Important: If water is unavailable or harmful to health, Tayammum (dry ablution) can substitute. Strike clean earth with your hands and wipe your face and arms.

Lifestyle & Community

🍽️ Halal Food

Avoid pork, alcohol, and non-halal meat. Look for halal certification. Seafood and vegetarian options are safe.

👔 Islamic Dress

Modesty: loose-fitting clothes. Women may choose hijab. Men cover from navel to knee.

🤝 Community

Visit your local mosque, join study circles, find a mentor. Building connections is key to your journey.

❤️ Character

Truthfulness, patience, kindness, humility, gratitude. The Prophet said: "I was sent to perfect good character."
